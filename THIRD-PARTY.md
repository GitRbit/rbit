## Fonts

None are bundled. The stylesheet asks for Geist / Inter and falls back to the system's own
fonts; nothing is downloaded from a font service.

## Rust dependencies

Crates from crates.io are compiled into the binary under their own licenses: MIT,
Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, Unicode, CDLA-Permissive and similar permissive terms, plus
MPL-2.0 for three crates used unmodified (`cssparser` and `dtoa-short` through `ammonia`,
`uluru` through `gix`). The allowed set is pinned in [deny.toml](deny.toml) and checked in
CI with `cargo deny check licenses`; `cargo deny list` prints the license of every crate in
the tree. Notable embedded data: `syntect` ships syntax definitions and themes derived from
Sublime Text packages (MIT-style terms), `webpki-roots` carries Mozilla's CA certificate
list (CDLA-Permissive-2.0), and the ICU crates carry Unicode data (Unicode-3.0).
